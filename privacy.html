Privacy Notice

Last updated: 7 November 2026

Who we are

ITwiz is an independent software studio. It is the trading name of an individual developer based in Algeria, who is the controller of the personal data described below.

What we collect

When you send a project brief through this site, we receive your name, your email address, the project type you select, and the message you write. We do not ask for sensitive information — please don't include medical, financial, or other sensitive details in your message.

Our hosting provider also records standard technical data (IP address, browser type, time of request) for security and abuse prevention.

Why we use it

Only to read and reply to your brief, and to prepare a proposal if you ask for one.

Legal bases: taking steps at your request before entering a contract (GDPR Art. 6(1)(b)), and our legitimate interest in keeping the site secure and free of abuse (Art. 6(1)(f)).

We do not use your data for marketing, profiling, or automated decision-making. We do not sell or rent it.

Who processes it

  • Netlify hosts this site and stores form submissions on our behalf.
  • Google (Gmail) delivers the email notification of your submission to us.

Both process data on our instructions under their standard data processing terms. Their infrastructure is outside Algeria and may be outside the EU/EEA; transfers rely on standard contractual clauses. Netlify's privacy policy: https://www.netlify.com/privacy/

No one else receives your data.

How long we keep it

If we don't start a project together, we delete your brief within 12 months. We review stored submissions every quarter and remove anything past that point. If we do work together, we keep the relevant records for the contract's duration and for as long as the law requires afterwards.

Your rights

You can ask us to show you what we hold, correct it, delete it, stop using it, or send it to you or another provider.

To make a request, use the contact form on this site and write “data request” in the message. We reply within one business day and act within one month.

If you think we've handled your data badly, you can complain to a supervisory authority — in Algeria, the ANPDP; in the EU, the authority in your country.

Cookies and local storage

This site sets no cookies and runs no analytics, advertising, or tracking scripts. Your browser stores two preferences locally — your chosen language, and whether you dismissed the mascot. They stay on your device and are never sent to us.

Security

The site is served over HTTPS. Access to submissions is limited to the studio and protected by account authentication.

Children

This site is intended for businesses and adults. It is not directed at anyone under 16.

Changes

If this notice changes, we update the date at the top.

Law

We handle personal data under Algerian Law 18-07 on the protection of personal data, and — for visitors in the European Union — the GDPR. Where the two differ, we apply the stricter rule.

Language

This notice is published in English, French, and Arabic. In case of discrepancy, the French version prevails.
