Checklist Audit — Field Inspections

Checklist Audit — Field Inspections

Constraint
Inspectors work in kitchens, workshops and warehouses with no reliable internet — and a failed item has to become a tracked corrective action, not a note in a notebook.
Decision
A no-build-step PWA whose database lives in a folder the inspector owns, through the File System Access API. When the browser can't, it falls back to IndexedDB, then localStorage. A service worker keeps the app itself offline.
Shipped
Zones and criteria, inspections with pass thresholds, non-conformities with owners and deadlines, photo evidence, PDF reports, and compliance trends over time. Ships as a PWA and a Capacitor Android build, in English and French.

Vanilla JS · Service Worker · IndexedDB · Capacitor

Cultural & Sports Hub — Scheduling

Cultural & Sports Hub — Scheduling

Constraint
Each chamber runs a weekly schedule, but a one-off event has to override an activity without destroying it — and deleting the event must restore the original.
Decision
Compute the effective calendar on the fly: expand the recurrence rules, subtract per-occurrence cancellations, then subtract overriding events. The underlying schedule is never mutated.
Shipped
Electron and embedded SQLite, with day, week and month views. Per-occurrence or whole-series edits, conflict detection that splits partial overlaps, and 24 unit tests over the engine.

Electron · SQLite · FullCalendar · esbuild

InventoryApp — Stock & Invoicing

InventoryApp — Stock & Invoicing

Constraint
A shop's stock, purchases, customer debts and invoices all have to agree — and the person at the counter shouldn't see the admin screens.
Decision
A Kotlin + Compose Desktop app with an embedded Nitrite document store. Categories use materialized paths, so a subtree's stock count is one prefix scan; invoices snapshot their lines, so a later price change never rewrites history.
Shipped
Eleven screens — dashboard, inventory, checkout, invoices, customers, providers, purchase orders, debts, returns, scanner, settings. PDF invoices, local backups, permission-gated navigation, English, French and Arabic UI. Windows installer.

Kotlin · Compose Desktop · Nitrite · PDFBox
